Hyafil Lab at the CRM

We study how neural mechanisms shape perception and decision-making using the interplay between theory (probabilistic modelling, dynamical systems, etc.) and experiments in humans, non-human primates and rodents. Click on ‘Research’ to learn more about our different lines of investigation. Together with the labs of Alex Roxin and Klaus Wimmer we form the Computational Neuroscience Unit at the CRM near Barcelona. We’re happy members of the Barccsyn community.
